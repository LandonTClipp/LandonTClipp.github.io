title: The Dawn of Break

<div class="poem-text">

For seven days the mountains kept 
Their shoulder to the rain
While pines stood dark and tall
And drank it all again.

No antler moved there among the reed.
No shadow crossed the stone.
The evening lowered without a word
And left me there alone.

I walked the miles she offered me,
Brown grass and aspen bark
The empty road, the ashen sky,
The early-coming dark.

She never answered when I asked,
Nor hurried for my sake.
The wind moved coldly through the firs
Like snow inside that brings the ache.

So many who have walked this road 
Will turn before they wake; 
Impatience from the forthright heart 
Will steal away the dawn of break.

But once, beside a shoulder pressed to mine, 
We watched the frozen upper ridges shine; 
Then emerald fire unfolded wings of light 
And brushed the stars like feathers through the night.

But once a black bear stepped from aspen gold,
Then vanished through the pines like smoke grown cold;
Three times beneath the dusk’s deep-purple sky,
The same two moose appeared, then wandered by.

And once I saw the early alpenglow,
And oh the flame did dance among the snow.
It wicked the silver mountain top with deepened red
And poured down every ridge where daylight bled.

And once again that promise did return,
The colors after rain began to burn;
A stroke of paint left colors in its train
And signed its name across the highland plain.

And in that rain, I called again to mind
The fleeting beauty weather leaves behind.
The air may dim, the bitter winds may blow;
Life’s weather lingers, sometimes slow to go.

So still I take the common trail
Where nothing waits for me,
Through quiet miles of dust and grass,
Past every vacant tree.

For she will keep her secrets close
And choose her time and place;
No hand can pull the bear from shade
Nor turn the heavens’ face.

I only know to leave the door,
To walk, to watch, to wait;
To be beneath the open sky
When something crosses late.

And should the whole day offer none
Of what I hoped to find,
I’ll sleep among the silent hills
And leave the lamp behind.
