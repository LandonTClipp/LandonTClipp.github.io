title: The Empty Side

<div class="poem" markdown>

<div class="poem-text">

One of those warm and ghostly nights,
    I lay back down.
The winds that pulled my roots asunder
    Quieted underground.

The weight beside my own
    Would surely never turn.
That comfort had always been
    A little thing I never earned.

The shape beside the chair that cast
    A shadow on the wall
Would break the torment of the seas
    And quiet every squall.

The sound I heard each evening there,
    Still pining for one more,
Was like a breeze across the waste
    The derecho made of my shore.

What am I to do but remember
    As though I couldn't see
The waves that broke beyond the rock
    And crashed right into me?

Who am I to save the day gone by
    As though the lightning never came
And struck the face that I still touch
    And scarred it with a flame?

I fear that every hand I hold
    Must carry the weight of my ache,
As though one smile could heal the haze
    That many years were slow to make.

My thoughts drift on to days out yonder
    And reach for something new.
One star has faded with the dawn;
    The next must see the morning dew.

Nothing more to say of what it was,
    Never more shall I see
The sunset that met my shore
    Without the hail upon my sea.
