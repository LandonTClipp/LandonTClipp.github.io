<div class="poem-text">

A small thing stirred beneath the bough,
Then vanished where I can’t see now.
A flash of feather, brief and bright,
Half-hidden in the folds of light.

The branch bent low, then settled still,
As evening gathered on the hill.
The mountains wore their shades of blue,
And all the world felt split in two.

The roads ahead dissolved in haze,
The roads behind were ghostly ways.
I stood between them, cold and numb,
Unsure of which was home to come.

The map we drew beside the fire,
Each hopeful town, each small desire.
The roads we traced through mountain rain,
Now drift like smoke I cannot claim.

The porch that faced a western view,
The winter mornings meant for two.
The names we never got to say,
Like birds at dusk, have flown away.

Some dreams depart without a sound,
Like roots that loosen underground.
No storm arrives, no trumpet calls,
Just silence moving through the walls.

A cup left out beside the sink,
A name that rises when you think.
A place reserved beside the fire,
Still warm from some forgotten pyre.

There hangs within the dark a room,
Untouched by dust, untouched by doom.
A younger man still waits inside,
Certain that love will be his guide.

He does not know what years can take,
Or how a trusting heart can break.
He only knows the stars above,
And all the simple faith of love.

The cruelest grief is not the past,
But futures that were built to last.
Not what was held, but what remained
Just out of reach, unnamed, unclaimed.

For every loss that time can show,
A hundred others never grow.
And in their absence lingers still
The quiet ache of what they will.

And somewhere in the deepening green,
A bird sleeps where it can’t be seen.
Perhaps it knows what I do not—
How much is lost, how much is sought.

The stars emerge, remote and far,
Each one a cold and ancient scar.
The darkness stretches, vast and tall,
And offers no replies at all.

Yet in the night a flame remains,
A fragile guard against the plains.
It shows no more than where I stand,
One patch of earth, one span of land.

Beyond its reach the shadows dwell,
Beyond its reach no tongue can tell.
But step by step, though small the sight,
The candle keeps me through the night.

And if beyond that narrow glow
There waits a road I do not know,
It is enough, for now, to see
The ground that’s been entrusted me.

The stars may wander. Dreams may part.
The dark may press against the heart.
But still the little flame burns bright—

One step.

Then one.

And then the night.
