<div class="poem-text">
Tonight I sit beneath the stars bright,
Beneath a campfire dark tonight.
Tucked amongst the mountains high,
Beneath the vast and silent sky.

The pines dissolve where once they swayed,
the blue-gray ridges blur and fade.
The distant valleys sink from view,
like folded cloth soaked dark with dew.

They still exist beyond my sight,
I simply cannot see tonight.
All I have is candle flame, 
A single light, yet just the same.

A single flame beside me glows,
and flickers softly as it throws
A little circle on the ground,
Revealing fragments all around.

A mud-stained boot within the light,
A camp chair resting in the night.
The edge of a table shows
Its weathered surface amber-glows.

Surrounded by the dark unknown,
Where blackened sloes stand cold and lone.
And starting at its gentle gleam,
I find myself begin to dream.

This is my life, I softly say,
And wonder what may come my way.
I want to know where roads may bend,
And where the winding pathways end.

I want to know if love will stay,
Or slowly drift and fade away.
I want to know what waits ahead,
What joys may bloom, what tears be shed.

Will healing come or regret remain,
Will loneliness or peace attain?
But still the candle will not tell,
It keeps its secrets far too well.

It offers neither map nor sign,
Nor guarantee of what's devine.
It gives enough to take one stride,
And leaves the rest concealed outside.

And truthfully, I hate it so,
For all the things it will not show.
I want a sunrise vast and bright,
I want great floodlights in the night.

I want the mountains bending near,
To whisper words I long to hear:
"Here is what happens, do not fear,
The ending of your tale is clear."

Instead there's silence all around,
No owl's reply, no guiding sound.
And only still the small flame stays,
Unmoved by all my pleading ways.

Patient there beside my chair,
As though it knows what lingers there.
As if it understands somehow,
What every life must learn by now.

No human soul has ever known,
A floodlit path, completely shown.
Only candles through the night,
Giving just enough of light.

One step taken, then one more,
Then another as before.
Perhaps that's all we've ever done,
Beneath the moon, beneath the sun.

Standing in the dark alone,
Holding candles of our own.
Trusting what the flame reveals,
Though hiding far more than it yields.

Not because it lights it all,
Or makes every burden small.
Not because it ends all pain,
Or keeps us safe from loss again.

But because it softly shows,
The next small place our footstep goes.
The mountains wait beyond my sight,
Invisible within the night.

The camp is still, the darkness deep,
And countless ridges drown in sleep.
Yet on the table, clear and slight,
The candle lifts its shard of light.

And when the wax burns low at last,
Its glow still trembling in the draft,
I watch one ember-colored thread,
Hold back the dark where I'm instead.
